# Why the gpt-6.1-terra baseline fails on ohr_dev_v1, and how much each repair could gain

Generated by `scripts/analysis/ohr_dev_v1_failures/analyse.py` from `results.json` (sha256 `ab6f32aef68d`). Rerun that command to regenerate it. Development data only: 70 questions from 30 documents, not a scientific evaluation.

## What is measured, what is interpretation, what is unverified

- **Measured** (from run records and fixed formulas): official EM and F1, ANLS, page recall@k, retrieval LCS, edit distance between MinerU and the clean reference text, and every headroom score, interval and test below.
- **Interpretation** (one coder's judgement): every code in `coding_agent_v2.csv` (codebook v2), so the failure breakdown, the evidence-impact counts and the repair a case points to. The coder is an AI agent, not a person. No second labeller has coded the cases yet, so agreement is unknown.
- **Unverified**: whether the codes agree with a human labeller; whether results hold beyond these 70 questions; how the undated `gpt-6.1-terra` alias behaves on a rerun; the image token price of that model; and the exact edit-distance normalisation behind the OHR-Bench paper's MinerU number, which its released code does not contain.
- **Pending**: headroom runs not yet completed: `gold_page_image`, `gold_page_image_only`. Their rows below say so.

## 1. Baseline

Run `2026-10-31-ohr-dev-v1-baseline-terra-r1`: 70 questions, EM 0.214, F1 0.468, ANLS 0.380. 15 exact, 32 partial (F1 > 0), 23 zero. The 55 non-exact cases are coded below.

Read with the coding caveat above: 27 of 55 failures are correct answers that EM scores 0, and 7 more rest on a questionable gold answer or question. OCR is the primary cause in 13 cases (10 lost, 3 damaged), retrieval in 2 (1 page-level, 1 passage-level), and the model in 6. If the coder's correctness judgements hold, 42 of 70 questions (60%) were answered correctly, against an official EM of 21.4%.

The three observations of study brief section 3, kept apart (counts over the 55 failures):

- **Text defect** (page image against MinerU, several labels per case allowed): `word_corruption` 27, `adequate` 15, `lost_structure` 13, `missing_content` 11.
- **Evidence impact** (does the needed passage survive in MinerU): `survives` 42, `damaged` 3, `lost` 10, `uncertain` 0.
- **Repair effect**: measured only by the headroom runs in section 4, not by the codes.
- **Gold and question flags**: `gold_questionable` 12, `question_ambiguous` 6, `gt_transcription_error` 2.

A damaged page does not mean a failure caused by OCR. Of the cases whose evidence survives, many sit on pages with lost structure or word corruption, and the model still found the passage.

## 3. Measurements per case

### Retrieval

Page recall over all 70 questions (no request counts as 0): @1 0.77, @3 0.87, @5 0.88. Only the 19 questions on multi-page documents test page choice; for them recall is @1 0.53, @3 0.89, @5 0.92. On single-page documents page recall is 1 by construction.

Passage coverage, over the 63 questions that sent a request: OHR-Bench's own retrieval metric (word-level LCS of the gold evidence context in the retrieved text, copied from upstream `lcs_score`) averages 0.54 for the 5 chunks sent and 0.49 for the top 2. Upstream splits on spaces, so unspaced Chinese text and MinerU's dropped spaces score near 0 even when the passage is there. The same ratio over characters (FAAR-defined) averages 0.94, and 53 of 70 questions pass the paper's 0.95 inclusion threshold on it. The character version agrees with the coder's `passage_in_prompt` judgement on most cases (see `case_measures.csv`). No lead-approved passage-matching rule exists yet (brief section 3), so treat both as diagnostics.

### OCR quality on the gold evidence pages

39 distinct evidence pages, 6 with empty MinerU text. Normalised edit distance (Levenshtein over the longer string, whitespace collapsed) averages 0.43 over all of them and 0.32 over non-empty pages (median 0.17). Character error rate (over the reference length) averages 0.64; it exceeds 1 where MinerU invents text. Pages with Chinese text average 0.67, the others 0.38.

| Domain | Mean NED |

| academic | 0.30 |

By evidence impact, mean CER is 0.22 where the evidence survives, 0.94 where it is damaged and 1.57 where it is lost. Page-level edit distance is a weak signal for one question, though. On the newspaper pages the clean reference itself has duplicated column fragments and character errors that MinerU does not, so a high distance there partly measures the reference, not MinerU.

### Softer answer score

ANLS (threshold 0.5, lowercase, FAAR-defined secondary metric) is 0.380 against EM 0.214 and F1 0.468. ANLS gives no credit to long correct answers, because it compares whole strings, so it does not recover the scoring artifacts either: a unit suffix on a short number (`108幅` against `108`) costs half the string. Neither F1 nor ANLS is a correctness judgement.

## 5. Sample size for a 5-point EM gain

For a paired comparison of a repair policy with the baseline on the same questions, McNemar's test at two-sided alpha 0.05 and power 0.8 needs the number of questions below to detect an EM gain of 5 points. The answer depends on how often the two policies disagree (the discordant rate). The second column inflates it by the document design effect estimated from the baseline (ICC of EM within documents 0.40, mean 2.3 questions per document, design effect 1.53).

| Discordant rate | Questions | With design effect |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1.4% | n/a | n/a |
| 6.0% | 186 | 285 |
| 10.0% | 312 | 477 |
| 11.4% | 356 | 544 |
| 15.0% | 469 | 717 |
| 20.0% | 626 | 957 |

Observed discordant rates against the baseline: (a) Clean ground-truth text, same retrieval 11.4%, (c) All MinerU text of the gold page(s), no retrieval 1.4%. A real repair touches fewer questions than an oracle condition, so its discordant rate is likely lower. A lower rate needs fewer questions for the same gain, but a real repair's gain is also likely smaller than 5 points.

The pilot's 70 questions cannot detect a 5-point gain at any of these rates.

## 6. Comparison with the OHR-Bench paper

Zhang et al., *OCR Hinders RAG* (ICCV 2025), Table 2, report MinerU with page edit distance 0.24 (ground truth has none), retrieval LCS 50.1 over all evidence types against 70.0 for ground truth, generation F1 36.7 against 43.9 with the gold page given, and overall F1 30.0 against 36.1 with retrieval. The relative overall loss from MinerU is about 17%.

| | OHR-Bench paper (MinerU) | This analysis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OCR edit distance | 0.24 (mean per page; normalisation not in the released code) | 0.32 mean NED over the 33 non-empty evidence pages, 0.43 with the 6 empty pages counted as 1 |
| Retrieval LCS | 50.1 (all types, top-2) | 44.0 (same formula, top-2, all 70; 48.6 top-5) |
| Overall F1, MinerU | 30.0 | 46.8 |
| Overall F1, clean text | 36.1 | 54.4 |
| Generation F1 with the gold page, MinerU | 36.7 | 50.8 |

The setups differ in ways that move every number, so only the direction of the MinerU-to-clean gap is comparable:

- **Answer model.** The paper averages Qwen2-7B and Llama-3.1-8B; this run uses `gpt-6.1-terra`, a much larger model, with a short-answer prompt.
- **Retrieval.** The paper searches a whole domain's knowledge base (BGE-M3 and BM25, averaged) and passes the top 2 chunks of 1,024 tokens. This run searches only the question's own document (23 of 30 are single pages) with a hybrid retriever and passes 5 chunks of 180 words.
- **Questions.** The paper uses all 8,498 questions across five evidence types. This pilot has 70 train-split questions, no charts, 22 Han-script questions, and 6 questions whose evidence page MinerU left empty.
- **Metrics.** The paper reports F1 (and LCS for retrieval) and drops blank answers before averaging; this report keeps every question, counts abstentions as 0 and adds EM and ANLS.
- **MinerU version.** Both use the MinerU output distributed with OHR-Bench, whose version and settings are unknown.

## 7. Limitations

- One agent coded all cases. The codebook was drafted after reading the cases, then fixed before coding; that is normal for a first codebook but means the categories fit this sample.
- 70 questions from 30 documents, chosen by a purposive quota. Counts describe this sample, not OHR-Bench.
- The clean reference text is OHR-Bench's transcription. On several pages it is worse than MinerU, and some gold answers and one question copy its errors.
- Headroom conditions use gold labels or clean text, so they bound a repair from above; they are not a repair.
- `gpt-6.1-terra` is an undated alias. All runs used it on 2026-10-31.
